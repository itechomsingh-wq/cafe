Veg, Vegan and Jain friendly

Bayleaf is fully vegetarian, but never “only paneer and potatoes”. You’ll find:

  • Clearly marked vegan dishes with plant-based milks, cheeses and sauces
  • Proper Jain options where the recipe is rethought, not just “remove onion-garlic”

So the vegan friend, the Jain colleague and the “I eat everything” cousin can all order happily from the same menu.
